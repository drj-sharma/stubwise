Privacy policy

Effective date this goes live. This page describes the CrewStub app for iPhone and iPad and this website.

CrewStub helps small employers make pay stubs and keep payroll records. To do that it stores information about your business and your workers. This page says what that information is, what we do with it and what you can do about it.

Who we are

your name or company name and address runs CrewStub. Questions about this policy go to crewstub.support@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account. The email address you sign up with, and a sign-in identifier. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifier and email address they share with us. If you use a password, we store only a one-way hash of it, never the password itself.

Your business. Its name, address, state, the logo you choose, and settings such as your pay schedule and stub design.

Your workers. Name, address, an email address if you add one, pay type and rate, the tax form answers you enter (such as W-4 answers and the state's form), amounts already paid this year, paid time off balances and any standing deductions. If you choose, you can add the last four digits of a worker's Social Security number, so they print on the stub as XXX-XX-1234. We never ask for or store a full Social Security number.

Pay stubs. The hours, pay lines, deductions, taxes, totals and dates on every stub you make, and any orders you place in the app.

Technical records. Our server keeps request logs, such as the time, the kind of request, your account identifier and an error code if something failed, so we can keep the service working and investigate problems. If you allow reminders, your phone schedules them on the device itself.

Photos. If you choose a logo, the app asks permission to read the one photo you pick. It reads nothing else from your photo library.

What we use it for

  • To run the app: show your workers and stubs on any device you sign in to, work out withholding and year-to-date totals, and make your reports and PDFs.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To fix problems, and to answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and the app does not include advertising or analytics tools that follow you across other apps or sites.

Who can see it

Only you, through your account, and the people who run CrewStub when they need to keep the service working or answer a support request you sent. We share information with others only in these cases:

  • Our hosting. Your records are stored on servers we run or rent. They are sent between your device and the server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Apple and Google, only if you choose to sign in with them. They handle your sign-in under their own policies.
  • The law. If we are legally required to, we will disclose what the law demands, and we will tell you where we are allowed to.

When you use "Send to worker," the app opens your phone's mail composer with the PDF attached. The email is sent from your own mail account. It does not pass through CrewStub's servers.

How long we keep it

We keep your records while your account exists, so your stubs and year-to-date totals stay correct. Server logs are kept for number of days. If you delete your account, your account, business, workers and stubs are permanently deleted from our servers. Copies in backups are removed on the backup schedule, within number of days.

Your choices

  • See and export your data. Every stub can be shared as a PDF, and reports can be exported as spreadsheets.
  • Correct it. Edit any worker, stub or setting in the app.
  • Delete it. In the app, open Settings, then your account at the top, then Delete everything. This removes your account and all its records, on every device, and cannot be undone.
  • Ask us. You can also email crewstub.support@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you or to ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, and we will respect them.

Your workers' information

You decide what to enter about your workers, and you are responsible for having the right to do so. Please share this policy with them if they ask what happens to their details.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and stored on servers with restricted access. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password or sign in with Apple, and keep your phone's passcode on.

Children

CrewStub is for people who run a business. It is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
