Common questions

Does CrewStub pay my workers or file my taxes?

No. CrewStub makes pay stubs and keeps your records. You still pay your workers, deposit payroll taxes and file the forms that apply to you. A stub is only as correct as the hours, rates and forms you enter.

Which states are calculated?

Federal withholding, Social Security and Medicare are calculated, along with state tax for 37 states. Nine states have no wage income tax. In the remaining few, the stub has a line you fill in yourself and marks it clearly. Settings, then Tax settings, shows every figure the app uses for federal tax or any state.

Why does a stub show a different tax than my accountant expected?

Withholding depends on the worker's W-4 and state form. Open the worker, check those details, and compare with the agency's own calculator. If you still see a difference, email us the state, pay period and gross pay (not personal details) and we will look at it.

Can I fix a stub after I made it?

Yes. Open the stub and change the lines. Later stubs and year-to-date totals update to match, and fixing a mistake is free.

How do I get my records out?

Share any stub as a PDF. Reports, under Insights, give earnings and deductions for any range, as a PDF or a spreadsheet file, plus year-end totals for each worker.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete everything. This permanently deletes your account, your business, every worker and every pay stub from our servers, on every device. It cannot be undone, so share or save any PDFs you need first.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad. It needs a recent version of iOS.
